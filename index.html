Pac-Man is a world-famous arcade game loved by players of all ages. In this game, you control a cheerful yellow character who navigates through a maze, chomping down dots while avoiding four colorful, mischievous ghosts.

This website introduces everything you need to know about the game, including its rich history, the unique personalities of each ghost, and secret tricks to get a high score. Whether you are a beginner or a fan, you will find fun facts that make the game even more exciting.
